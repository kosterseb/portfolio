A small kid crosses a desert that doesn't want to be crossed. Deep sand slows you down, storms force you to dig in, and there's almost no interface: you figure things out by looking.

Under the hood it's a lot of systems quietly working together: sand that reacts to every step, a weather engine with sandstorms, a full day and night cycle, spatial audio that places every gust of wind, and controller support with vibration. The kid's tool, the Fumbrella, works as a shield, a board and a kite, and grows with you through the game.

I also write the music for it, which gets played sparingly, so the silence between the songs is part of the score.
