Wizflow is Dreamplan's no-code platform for digitalising finance, from the first click to the final digital signature. Instead of building sign-ups, questionnaires and customer journeys in code, teams put them together as flows on a visual canvas.

The newest part is the Wizflow Assistant. You describe what you want to create, attach a document if you have one, and the assistant builds the flow step by step on the canvas, then sums up everything it created. Ready-made prompts cover things like turning a static PDF into an interactive chat flow, or translating a flow into Norwegian.

I work on Wizflow's frontend, where the job is making something powerful feel calm: a canvas you can find your way around, an assistant that shows what it's doing while it works, and all the small states in between.
